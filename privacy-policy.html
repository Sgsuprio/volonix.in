Privacy Policy

VOLONIX · Effective date: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

VOLONIX is a small business based in India that sells mobile accessories online. Our website is https://volonix.in. This Privacy Policy explains how our Pinterest application, “Muse VOLONIX”, handles information when it connects to Pinterest.

2. What the app does

“Muse VOLONIX” is a tool that helps us publish Pins to our own Pinterest business account. It reads our own boards and Pins, and creates new Pins from our own product photos and content. It only ever accesses our own Pinterest account — never any other Pinterest user’s account, boards, or Pins.

3. Information the app accesses

When we connect the app to Pinterest, Pinterest asks us for permission to access:

  • Our Pinterest account information (account name and profile details);
  • Our boards (to list them and choose where a Pin goes);
  • Our Pins (to read them and to create new ones).

The app uses the Pinterest API with the following permissions only: boards:read, pins:read, pins:write, and user_accounts:read. We do not request access to any other Pinterest data.

4. How the information is used

The information listed above is used only to:

  • Show us our own boards so we can choose where to publish;
  • Create and schedule Pins on our own account;
  • Confirm that a Pin was published.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not access, collect, or store any other Pinterest user’s data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use the app for advertising, tracking, or profiling.
  • We do not collect Pinterest passwords. Sign-in happens on Pinterest’s own pages.

6. How access tokens are stored

The app connects to Pinterest using OAuth. The access tokens Pinterest issues are stored securely by our assistant platform and are used only to make the API calls described above. If we disconnect the app, the tokens are revoked and no longer work.

7. Data retention

We keep no copy of Pinterest data beyond what is needed to publish a Pin. We do not keep a database of Pinterest users or their activity.

8. Children’s privacy

Our app is a business tool for our own account. It is not directed at children and does not collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our website at https://volonix.in.
